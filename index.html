About Hotel Bella Cafe & Restaurant

Hotel Bella Cafe & Restaurant is a family-run hotel, restaurant and cafe on Mangaldeep marg, Ward 5, Damak, in Jhapa district, eastern Nepal. The property brings together five guest rooms, a full restaurant kitchen, a coffee bar and a banquet hall used for weddings and corporate functions across Jhapa.

The kitchen cooks Nepali, Indian, continental and Asian dishes in one menu. Nepali plates include chicken choila, sekuwa, thukpa, mustard fish curry and Nepali-style mutton curry. Continental and Asian options run from Margherita pizza and carbonara to biryani, sizzlers and tandoori breads baked to order. Most main dishes fall between Rs 150 and Rs 550. The coffee bar serves espresso drinks, shakes, lassi and smoothies, with the KitKat milkshake and sizzling brownie among the most ordered items.

Guest rooms range from a Standard Room at Rs 3,500 per night to a Family Room sleeping five at Rs 5,500. Every room has air conditioning, an attached bathroom, a smart TV and free Wi-Fi. Deluxe rooms and above include a coffee maker, and the Suite adds a jacuzzi, separate living area and mini bar.

The property has free parking, wheelchair ramps and accessible seating, outdoor tables, a kids' menu with high chairs, and live music by local artists on evenings. Cards, mobile wallets and cash are all accepted.

Damak sits on the East-West Highway, which makes the hotel a practical overnight stop between Kathmandu and Kakarbhitta, and a base for Ilam's tea gardens, Pathibhara Devi Temple and Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve.

Places to Visit Near Damak

Damak is a convenient base for eastern Nepal. Kanchenjunga, the third-highest peak in the world, is visible on clear days. Biratnagar is about 1.5 hours by road, Baraha Kshetra about 2.5 hours, Pathibhara Devi Temple and Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve about 4 hours each, and the Ilam tea gardens about 5 hours.
